## Description

ChunkHP makes damage *feel* like damage. When your target or a nameplate takes a hit, the health it lost flashes, then lingers as a "ghost" chunk that drains away. It's the delayed health bar you know from fighting games, Soulslikes and JRPGs, on WoW Forever's own frames.

**Features**
- Works on your **target frame** and **nameplates**.
- **Flash** on the lost health, and a **draining trail** behind it.
- **Styles:** Classic MMO, Soulslike, Fighting Game, JRPG and Arcade, or tune your own.
- **Live preview** in the options, drawn like a real nameplate in your Nameplate Style and Size, with Hit / Big hit / Heal / Auto buttons.
- **Damage Numbers** shortcuts: turn damage, damage-over-time and healing numbers on targets on or off, and change their size where the client supports it.
- Built for WoW Forever's secret health values: it never reads or calculates health, so it doesn't cause errors.

**Setup**
Esc > Options > AddOns > ChunkHP, or type `/chunkhp`.

**Known limits**
- Enemy nameplates inside dungeons and raids can't be changed by addons, so only your target frame shows the effect there.
- The trail's drain speed is fixed by the game.
